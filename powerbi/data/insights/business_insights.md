# Business Insights

## 1. Sales Trend and Seasonality

Total revenue increased from 377,563.22 in 2024 to 393,510.96 in 2025, representing approximately 4.22% year-over-year growth.

Monthly performance fluctuated considerably across both years rather than following a consistent upward trend.

In 2024, revenue peaked in December at 36,456.28, while October recorded the lowest revenue at 22,384.22.

In 2025, August recorded the highest monthly revenue at 39,507.55, while June recorded the lowest at 23,175.69.

The data suggests some periods of stronger and weaker sales, but the pattern is not strong enough to conclude that there is a consistent seasonal cycle. Further analysis over additional years would be required to confirm seasonality.

## 2. Product Performance

Shampoo was the highest-revenue product, generating approximately 49,818.44 in revenue.

Other strong-performing products included:

- Stapler
- Desk Organizer
- Women's Dress
- Blender
- Electric Kettle
- Face Cream
- Smartwatch

This suggests that both Beauty & Personal Care and Office Supplies contain important revenue-generating products.

## 3. Category Performance

Beauty & Personal Care was the highest-performing category, generating approximately 195,596.39 in revenue.

The category ranking was:

1. Beauty & Personal Care — 195,596.39
2. Office Supplies — 168,944.18
3. Electronics — 148,231.18
4. Home & Kitchen — 135,657.91
5. Fashion — 122,644.52

Beauty & Personal Care therefore represents an important category for maintaining and expanding revenue performance.

---

## 4. Customer Segment Performance

Wholesale customers generated the highest revenue at approximately 285,035.37.

Wholesale also recorded the highest quantity purchased and highest order volume.

This indicates that wholesale customers represent an important high-value customer segment and may deserve stronger retention and relationship-management strategies.

## 7. Product Returns

The returns analysis identified Desk Organizer as having the highest number of recorded returns, with 10 returns.

Other products with relatively high return counts included:

- Leather Belt — 9
- Power Bank — 8
- Bluetooth Speaker — 8
- Women's Dress — 8
- Face Cream — 8
- Perfume — 8

These products should be monitored to determine whether return patterns are associated with product quality, customer expectations, pricing, or other operational factors.

---

## 8. Management Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Strengthen relationships with high-value wholesale customers.
2. Protect and expand the strongest geographical markets.
3. Investigate why Phone Order generates the highest revenue and order volume.
4. Prioritize high-performing products and categories.
5. Investigate products with relatively high return counts.
6. Monitor profitability alongside revenue to ensure that high sales translate into sustainable business performance.
7. Develop a management dashboard containing regularly monitored KPIs.

---

## 9. Key Business Takeaway

Management should investigate the factors behind high-performing months such as August 2025 and December 2024, as well as weaker periods such as October 2024 and June 2025. Understanding these fluctuations could help improve sales planning, inventory management, and promotional strategies.

The analysis shows that business performance is concentrated around specific products, customer segments, geographical markets, and sales channels.

The greatest opportunities are likely to come from strengthening high-value customer relationships, protecting leading markets, understanding successful sales channels, and improving the performance of products with weaker sales or higher return rates.
